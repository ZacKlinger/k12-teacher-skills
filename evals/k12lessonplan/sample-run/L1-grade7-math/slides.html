New word · we use this all period

Proportional means the same ratio in every row.

Word
Proportional
say it: pro-POR-shun-ul
proporcional
tỉ lệ thuận
What it means
Every row has the same ratio. Double one amount and the other doubles too.
This is not proportional
Bowling: a $3 shoe fee is added before you play a single game.
New word · every row has one

A ratio compares two amounts.

Word
Ratio
say it: RAY-shee-oh
razón
tỉ số
What it means
$3 for 2 snow cones. Cost compared with how many.
This is not a ratio
"Up $3 each row." That is a change from row to row, not a comparison inside one row.

New word · the test uses it

The unit rate is the amount for one.

Word
Unit rate
say it: YOO-nit rate
tasa unitaria
tỉ lệ đơn vị
What it means
The cost of one snow cone: $3.00 ÷ 2 = $1.50.
This is not a unit rate
"Up $3 each row." That is how much a row adds, not the cost of one.
The test · four steps

Divide every row. Same answer every time means proportional.

Step 1
Pick a row

Snow cones: 2 cones, $3.00.

→
Step 2
Cost ÷ number

3.00 ÷ 2 = 1.50

→
Step 3
Every row

6.00 ÷ 4 = 1.50 · 9.00 ÷ 6 = 1.50

→
Result
Same every row

Proportional. One cone costs $1.50.
