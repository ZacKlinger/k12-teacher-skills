Word

Key word · how we measure today

Flow rate is how much water comes out in one minute.

Word
Flow rate
say it: FLOW rate
caudal
Worked example
40 seconds to fill 1 liter: 60 ÷ 40 = 1.5 liters a minute.
Not a flow rate
"40 seconds." That is a time. Turn it into liters a minute.

Name it · what we found

The higher the water has to go, the less comes out.

The pump
Pushes

Same push every second.

→
The height
Takes some

Lifting water 120 cm uses part of the push.

→
The system
Less at the top

Lower flow rate up high. Every part matters.

If the top falls short · every fix costs something

Two ways to get more water to the top. Each one costs us something.

Fix 1

A stronger pump

More push. Costs more. Louder in class.

Fix 2

A lower top channel

Less lift. Room for fewer plants.

Key word · in the video

To prime a pump is to fill it with water and push the air out.

Word
Prime
say it: PRIME
cebar
Why
A pump full of air spins but moves almost no water.
Not priming
Turning the pump on in a dry tote. It runs, it heats up, nothing comes out.
